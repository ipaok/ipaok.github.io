# Ministry Teams Overview

IPA's tech and creative ministry is made up of several teams, each playing a distinct role in making Sunday services happen. Here's a quick overview of what each team does.

---

## Media — Live Stream

The Live Stream team extends our worship services beyond the physical walls of the church, reaching members and visitors wherever they are. This team manages the live broadcast of each service — operating cameras, managing audio levels, switching between video sources, and coordinating with the rest of the tech teams to deliver a professional and engaging stream.

When technical issues come up during a broadcast, this team troubleshoots in real time to keep the stream running without interruption. Their work is essential for connecting with people who can't attend in person and helping the online congregation feel like they're truly part of the service.

---

## Media — Sound

The Sound team is responsible for every aspect of audio during a service — from setup and sound check to mixing and breakdown. This includes managing the soundboard, microphones, monitors, and all other audio equipment. They balance levels for the worship band, speakers, and any other audio elements so that everything — every word, every note — is clear and well-balanced for both the room and the livestream.

The Sound team works closely with the worship and tech teams throughout the service, making real-time adjustments as needed. Their work creates the sonic environment that allows everyone in the room to fully engage.

---

## Creatives

The Creatives team communicates IPA's mission and culture through visual content — social media, graphic design, photography, videography, and more. They craft and share content throughout the week that keeps our community connected and inspired, and that introduces new people to our church.

Through eye-catching design, compelling storytelling, and consistent branding, the Creatives team ensures our message reaches people well beyond Sunday morning.

---

## Networking

The Networking team maintains the digital infrastructure that everything else depends on. They manage the church's internal network — Wi-Fi, internet connectivity, and the integration of all the devices and systems that power our services. They work behind the scenes to troubleshoot issues, maintain security, and optimize performance so that every other tech team can do their job without interruption.

From the live stream to the soundboard to the graphics station, reliable connectivity is the foundation — and the Networking team makes sure it's always there.

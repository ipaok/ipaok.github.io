# Graphic Design

Welcome to the Creative Team at IPA! We're thrilled to have you join us in our mission to spread the message through creative and engaging content. As part of this team, you'll play a key role in visually communicating the values and spirit of our church to our community and beyond.

## Our Design Tool: Canva

At IPA, we use **Canva** as our primary design tool. Canva is a user-friendly platform that makes it easy to create professional-looking designs — whether you're a total beginner or an experienced designer. We use it for social media graphics, flyers, presentations, and more.

### Getting Started with Canva

1. **Create an account** — Visit [canva.com](https://www.canva.com/) and sign up with your personal email address.
2. **Get team access** — Reach out to the Creative Lead (Jeff Mathew) or Media Director (Justin Thomas) to be added to the IPA Canva Team, where you'll find all our existing templates and brand assets.
3. **Explore templates** — Browse Canva's library of templates for flyers, social media posts, presentations, and more.
4. **Watch tutorials** — Canva has a great collection of tutorial videos on their website and YouTube channel to help you get comfortable with the platform.
5. **Try a practice project** — Create something simple to get a feel for the tools. Don't be afraid to experiment with layouts, colors, and elements.
6. **Ask for help** — No question is a dumb question. Reach out to a team member or leadership anytime.

## What Our Team Creates

- **Printed materials** — Brochures, banners, and signage for church events.

## Our Style Guide

A consistent visual identity helps IPA look polished and professional across every platform. As a team, we work together to maintain these standards:

1. **Color palette** — We use colors that reflect the spirit of IPA. Stick to the approved brand colors available in the IPA Canva Team.
2. **Typography** — Use the designated primary font for headings and secondary font for body text. Consistency in fonts makes everything look more cohesive.
3. **Imagery** — Use photography and graphics that feel authentic to our church. Real moments from our services and events are always preferred over generic stock images.
4. **Logo usage** — Always use the official IPA logo at the correct size and with proper clearance. Don't stretch, recolor, or alter the logo.
5. **Voice and tone** — Keep written content warm, welcoming, and on-brand. We're speaking to our community, not a corporate audience.
6. **Design elements** — Borders, patterns, and icons that are used consistently help reinforce our brand across different pieces.
7. **Accessibility** — Design with everyone in mind. Use sufficient color contrast, readable font sizes, and alt text for images when applicable.

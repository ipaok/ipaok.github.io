# Planning Center Services

Planning Center Services is the tool we use to organize Sunday services — scheduling volunteers, building service orders, planning the worship set, and more. If you're serving on any team at IPA, you'll use Planning Center to accept or decline your scheduling requests and find your assignments for each week.

The videos below are official Planning Center tutorials to help you get familiar with the platform.

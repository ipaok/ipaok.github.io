# FLC Live Stream Guide

## Introduction

Thank you so much for giving your time to serve on the Live Stream team! You may be working behind the scenes, but what you do genuinely matters — you're the connection point for everyone watching online.

This guide walks you through the full setup, operations, and shutdown process. If you'd like a more in-depth training session, reach out to the media director — this is a condensed overview of everything involved.

---

## Setup

### 1. Turn On the Video Rack

Locate the red switches on the video rack and flip them to the **On** position.

> The lyrics station usually handles this first — if the rack is already on, skip to step 2.

![](img/2024-08-12-17-50-18.png)

### 2. Grab the iPad and Head Downstairs to Turn On Cameras

Take the iPad with you — you'll need it to control Camera 2 and Camera 3 via their sliders.

### 3. Turn On Camera 1 — Main Camera (Back Center of FLC)

Flip the power switch located below the camera body.

![](img/2024-08-12-18-17-42.png)

### 4. Turn On Camera 2 — Stage View Camera (Back Left of FLC)

1. Press the power switch on the slider controller.
2. Flip the power switch on the camera itself.

![](img/2024-08-12-18-17-55.png)
![](img/2024-08-12-18-18-02.png)

### 5. Set Up the Slider for Camera 2 (on the iPad)

Open the slider control app on the iPad.

![](img/2024-08-12-18-25-22.png)
![](img/2024-08-12-18-25-35.png)

### 6. Turn On Camera 3 — Keys Cam (Right Side of Stage)

1. Press the power switch on the slider controller.
2. Flip the power switch on the camera itself.

![](img/2024-08-12-18-25-46.png)
![](img/2024-08-12-18-25-52.png)
![](img/2024-08-12-18-26-00.png)

> The images above show Camera 1 as an example — the process is the same for the Keys Cam.

### 7. Turn On Camera 4 — Drum Cam (Left Side of Stage)

Flip the power switch located below the camera.

![](img/2024-08-12-18-26-16.png)

### 8. Turn On the Mac Mini

The power button is on the back of the Mac Mini, located at the bottom of the video rack. Press it to power on.

![](img/2024-08-12-18-27-30.png)
![](img/2024-08-12-18-27-39.png)

### 9. Turn On the TV and Monitor

Once the computer is on, switch on both the TV and the monitor.

### 10. Launch ATEM and Web Presenter

After logging in, click the icons highlighted in red in the taskbar image below to launch the ATEM switcher software and the BlackMagic Web Presenter app.

![](img/2024-08-12-18-27-54.png)

### 11. Open YouTube via the Stream Deck

Press the **YouTube** button on the Stream Deck.

![](img/2024-08-12-18-28-03.png)

### 12. Create Today's Live Event and Copy the Stream Key

1. Click **Go Live**.
   ![](img/2024-08-12-18-28-17.png)

2. Click **Schedule Stream**.
   ![](img/2024-08-12-18-28-29.png)

3. Click **Reuse Settings**.
   ![](img/2024-08-12-18-28-39.png)

4. Update the date to today and click **Next**. The stream key will appear on the final screen.
   ![](img/2024-08-12-18-28-51.png)

5. **Copy the Stream Key** — you'll need it in the next step.
   ![](img/2024-08-12-18-29-00.png)

### 13. Paste the Stream Key into Web Presenter

1. Open the Web Presenter app and click the **Config** button.
   ![](img/2024-08-12-18-29-08.png)
   ![](img/2024-08-12-18-29-20.png)

2. Paste the stream key into the **Key** field and click **Save**.

### 14. Final Checks

- Confirm all four cameras are on.
- Make sure the Foldback TV on stage is turned on.
- Test each camera by pressing **Cam 1** through **Cam 4** on the Stream Deck to verify they're all working.

Setup is complete!

## Operations

1. Turn on the sliders for Camera 2 and Camera 3.

2. Press **PP7** and then **Cut** to bring up the ProPresenter output. Wait for the lyrics volunteer to display the countdown. Your screen should look like the image below — the countdown is highlighted in red, with Camera 2 in preview.
   ![](img/2024-08-12-18-29-37.png)

3. When the countdown appears on screen (around 10:26 AM), go live by opening the **Web Presenter Config**, pressing **On Air**, and clicking **Save**.
   ![](img/2024-08-12-18-29-48.png)

4. When the countdown finishes, press **Fade** to switch to Camera 2.

5. Frame the person doing the intro on Camera 1, then press **Cut** to switch to Camera 1.
   ![](img/2024-08-12-18-29-57.png)

6. As the intro wraps up, switch back to Camera 2 and reframe Camera 1 on whoever is leading the first song.
   ![](img/2024-08-12-18-30-06.png)

7. As soon as worship begins, double-press the **On Air** button to display lyrics on the live stream.

8. Switch between cameras naturally — follow the music, follow the energy. Think about what you'd want to see as an online viewer to feel like you're in the room.

9. When worship ends, double-press the **On Air** button again to remove the lyrics from the stream.

10. When the pastor or speaker takes the stage, switch to Camera 2 for a wide shot and use the next 30–50 seconds to reframe Camera 1 on the speaker.
    ![](img/2024-08-12-18-30-17.png)

11. Keep Camera 1 focused on the speaker. Some speakers move around a lot — others stay put. Feel free to cut to Camera 2 or Camera 3 for audience reaction shots to help online viewers feel connected.

12. When the lyrics volunteer displays scripture or sermon slides, mirror them on the stream by pressing **PP7** and cutting between slides and video. Try not to hold on a slide for more than 30–60 seconds at a time.

13. After the sermon, the worship team will return for a closing song — follow the same camera approach as during worship.

14. After the closing song, reposition Camera 1 on the pastor or whoever is leading the ending prayer.

15. During announcements, display slides using the **PP7** button and switch between slides and video just like the sermon.

16. To end the stream, wait for the lyrics volunteer to put up the outro video. Press **PP7** and **Cut** to send it to the stream.

17. Wait about 1 minute, then open the **BlackMagic Web Presenter HD Options**, set the stream to **Off**, and click **Save**.
    ![](img/2024-08-12-18-30-33.png)

You're done! Follow the shutdown steps below, clean up your station, and leave it ready for the next person.

## Shutdown

1. Close all open programs and shut down the computer properly.
   ![](img/2024-08-12-18-30-43.png)

2. Turn off all four cameras and power down their slider controllers.

3. Turn off the Foldback Screen (stage monitor).

4. Power down the video rack — flip the top switch off first, then the bottom.

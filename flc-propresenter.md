## Startup & Setup

### 1. Log In and Open ProPresenter

1. Complete the Pre-Startup Checklist above.
2. Log in with the password provided by the media director.
3. Open **ProPresenter (PP7)**.
   ![](img/2024-08-12-17-52-59.png)

### 2. Load This Week's Service from Planning Center

1. In the **Library** panel on the left, select **Sunday Services**, then click the **+** button and choose **Planning Center Service…**
   ![](img/2024-08-12-17-53-18.png)
   ![](img/2024-08-12-17-53-31.png)

2. Expand the **Sunday Combined Service** dropdown and select this week's date.
   ![](img/2024-08-12-17-54-12.png)

3. Click **Select** to load the schedule. It should appear in your playlist and match your Planning Center account. If anything looks off, right-click the Sunday Service playlist and choose **Refresh**.
   ![](img/2024-08-12-17-54-24.png)

### 3. Add the Countdown Slides

Drag the **Countdown Slides** from the library into the countdown slot in your playlist.

![](img/2024-08-12-17-54-36.png)
![](img/2024-08-12-17-54-44.png)

### 4. Add the Welcome Slide

Drag the **Master Slides** into the Intro slot and select **"Welcome."**

![](img/2024-08-12-17-55-01.png)
![](img/2024-08-12-17-55-12.png)
![](img/2024-08-12-17-55-19.png)

### 5. Add Songs to the Playlist

Drag each song from the library into its designated spot in the playlist. If a song isn't in the library yet, import it from CCLI SongSelect. (See the [Adding Songs](adding-songs-propresenter.md) guide for instructions.)

![](img/2024-08-12-17-55-36.png)

### 6. Add Backgrounds to Each Song

Add a background video to each song. Match the energy of the background to the tempo and feel of the song — slower songs get slower, more atmospheric backgrounds; high-energy songs get faster ones.

### 7. Download and Import the Sermon Slides

1. Log into the media email and download the pastor's sermon file.
   ![](img/2024-08-12-17-56-10.png)

2. In ProPresenter, go to **File > Import > PowerPoint…**
   ![](img/2024-08-12-17-56-22.png)

3. Select the sermon file and click **Import**.
   ![](img/2024-08-12-17-56-40.png)

4. In the Library Selection window, choose **Sermon Slides** from the dropdown and click **OK**.
   ![](img/2024-08-12-17-56-58.png)

5. Go back to your playlist and drag the sermon slides into today's service.
   ![](img/2024-08-12-17-57-13.png)

### 8. Add the Kids' Dismissal Slide

Find the **Kids Dismissal** slide in the Presentations library and add it to the playlist in the appropriate spot.

### 9. Download and Add Announcement Slides

1. Open Canva from the desktop icon or the link below and download this week's announcement slides.
   ![](img/2024-08-12-17-58-28.png)
   ![](img/2024-08-12-17-58-41.png)
   ![](img/2024-08-12-17-58-49.png)

2. Open the **Announcements Master** in ProPresenter.
   ![](img/2024-08-12-17-59-02.png)

3. Delete all slides **except the first one**.
   ![](img/2024-08-12-17-59-11.png)
   ![](img/2024-08-12-17-59-18.png)

4. Drag this week's announcement slides (all slides except the first) into the Announcements Master.
   ![](img/2024-08-12-17-59-25.png)
   ![](img/2024-08-12-17-59-31.png)

### 10. Set Up Song Triggers (Actions)

For the **first song**, add the following triggers:

- **Start a countdown timer** — set it for 20 minutes with overrun allowed.
  ![](img/2024-08-12-17-59-56.png)
  ![](img/2024-08-12-18-00-05.png)

- **Set the Stage View** — make sure the Foldback and Multiview are both set to **Worship_IPA**.
  ![](img/2024-08-12-18-00-14.png)

### 11. Set Audience Looks

- Click on the **first slide of each song** and apply the Audience Look for songs.
  ![](img/2024-08-12-18-00-27.png)

- Apply the **Audience Look for the Sermon**.
  ![](img/2024-08-12-18-00-37.png)

- Apply the **Audience Look for Announcements**.
  ![](img/2024-08-12-18-00-44.png)

### 12. Set Up Sermon Triggers

On the first slide of the sermon, add the following triggers:

- **Start a 30-minute timer** with overrun allowed.
  ![](img/2024-08-12-18-01-03.png)
  Click **Time Left**, then set the timer to 30 minutes.
  ![](img/2024-08-12-18-01-21.png)

- **Set the Audience Look and Stage layout** for the sermon.
  ![](img/2024-08-12-18-01-37.png)
  ![](img/2024-08-12-18-01-51.png)
  Make sure **Presentation IPA** is selected for both stage layouts.

- **Change the theme** from **IPA Worship** to **IPA Sermon Slides**.
  ![](img/2024-08-12-18-02-07.png)

## Operations

- When you arrive, turn on **house lights #2 and #3**. Lights #1 and #4 stay off for the entire service.
  ![](img/2024-08-12-18-02-17.png)

- Open the **Church Mix** playlist on Spotify and have it ready to play.

- Start playing music on Spotify at **10:20 AM**.

- Launch the countdown at **10:26 AM**.

- At **4:00 on the countdown**, begin gradually lowering the Spotify volume as the countdown audio takes over.

- At **0:30 on the countdown**, turn off house lights #2 and #3.
  ![](img/2024-08-12-18-02-28.png)

- After the countdown ends, display the **Welcome** graphic and hold it until the worship team takes the stage.

- When the worship leader begins speaking or singing, click the first song's blank slide to start the "Worship Ends In" countdown timer.

- During worship, stay focused on the lyrics and advance slides in time with the music.

- When worship ends and the pastor steps up to welcome the congregation, turn house lights #2 and #3 back on.
  ![](img/2024-08-12-18-02-40.png)

- Just before the sermon begins, display the **"Children's Church Dismissed"** slide.

- During the sermon, advance through the pastor's slides as he teaches. If he's only referencing scripture, display those Bible verses in ProPresenter.

- When the sermon ends, switch to a blank slide as the worship leader returns.

- Stay on the blank slide through the pastor's closing prayer.

- When announcements begin, display the first announcement slide and cue up the Spotify Church Mix (keep it muted for now).

- Advance through the announcement slides as the speaker presents them.

- When the announcements speaker walks off stage, play the **outro video** and slowly bring up the Spotify volume.

That's the full rundown! Thank you for serving today — your work makes Sunday happen.

## Shutdown

1. Close **ProPresenter** on the Mac Studio.
2. Turn off all displays:
   - Press the power button on the TV.
   - Use the remote to turn off the projector.
   - Flip both red switches on the Livestream Rack to Off.
3. Shut down the **Mac Studio**.
4. Long-press the **UPS button** (behind the computer screen) until you hear beeping — this safely powers it down.
5. Clean up the desk and throw away any trash.

## FAQs

**What's the difference between the Library and a Playlist?**

- The **Library** is where all your media lives — slides, songs, videos, images, and presentations. Think of it as your master storage.
- A **Playlist** is the running order for a specific Sunday service. You pull items from the Library into a Playlist and arrange them in the order they'll appear during the service.
